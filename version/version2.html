Sacred Destinations

Explore Nepal's most revered pilgrimage sites, each with its own spiritual significance and ancient history.

Pashupatinath Temple

One of the most sacred Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Shiva, located on the banks of the Bagmati River in Kathmandu.

Lumbini

The birthplace of Lord Buddha, featuring the sacred Maya Devi Temple, ancient ruins, and peaceful meditation gardens.

Learn More

Muktinath Temple

A sacred place for both Hindus and Buddhists, nestled in the Himalayas with 108 water spouts and an eternal natural flame.

Boudhanath Stupa

One of the largest spherical stupas in Nepal, rich in Buddhist symbolism with the all-seeing eyes of Buddha gazing in four directions.
